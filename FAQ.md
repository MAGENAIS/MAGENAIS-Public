# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This document answers some of the most common questions about MAGENAIS.

For detailed technical documentation, architecture guides, and product information, please refer to the documentation available in the `docs/` directory.

---

# What is MAGENAIS?

MAGENAIS is a browser-native GENAI Operating System designed to unify modern Artificial Intelligence technologies into a single intelligent platform.

It provides a consistent environment for working with multiple AI capabilities including:

* Text Generation
* Vision
* Image Generation
* Video Generation
* Audio & Music
* Speech Processing
* Document Intelligence
* Data Analytics
* Research
* AI Agents
* Intelligent Workflows
* Browser AI
* Local AI
* Cloud AI

The platform is designed around a modular, provider-independent architecture that enables users to work with diverse AI technologies through one unified interface.

---

# Does MAGENAIS require a backend server?

No.

MAGENAIS is designed as a browser-native platform.

Most functionality executes directly within the browser.

Cloud AI providers can be connected using user-provided credentials when required.

Certain enterprise deployments or future commercial services may optionally include dedicated backend components.

---

# Can MAGENAIS work offline?

Yes.

Many platform capabilities are designed to operate locally whenever practical.

Depending on the selected AI provider and installed local models, numerous workflows can continue functioning without an active Internet connection.

Cloud-based services remain available whenever online connectivity is preferred.

---

# Which AI providers are supported?

MAGENAIS is designed to integrate with a wide range of AI providers through a unified provider architecture.

Supported providers may include commercial cloud services, browser-native AI, local models, enterprise infrastructure, and future AI technologies.

The platform is intentionally provider-independent, allowing users to choose the solutions that best meet their requirements.

---

# Can I switch providers without changing my projects?

Yes.

Provider independence is one of the fundamental architectural principles of MAGENAIS.

Projects and workflows are designed to remain portable whenever technically possible, allowing users to change providers without rebuilding their applications.

---

# Does MAGENAIS support Local AI?

Yes.

MAGENAIS supports Local AI, Browser AI, and Cloud AI through a unified execution architecture.

Users can select the most appropriate execution environment based on performance, privacy, hardware capabilities, and project requirements.
